A route, not a checklist

Walkthrough

Follow the main path, but explore side regions before each major turning point. Recommended levels are flexible: weapon upgrades and your own comfort matter just as much.

01 · LIMGRAVE

Find your footing

Activate Gatefront's Site of Grace to meet Melina and receive Torrent. Collect the Limgrave map, visit the Church of Elleh, and use the Stormveil gate route to reach Margit.

  • Optional early detour: Weeping Peninsula and Castle Morne.
  • Defeat Margit, then explore Stormveil Castle for its shortcuts and loot.
  • Suggested range: level 20–30; upgrade your weapon before the castle.
02 · LIURNIA

Reach the Academy

Enter Liurnia through Stormveil or take the cliff path around it. Find the Academy Glintstone Key west of Raya Lucaria, then defeat Rennala to unlock rebirth.

  • Explore Caria Manor and the Three Sisters to begin Ranni's quest.
  • Raya Lucaria is the next major Great Rune route.
  • Suggested range: level 40–60.
03 · CAELID

Attend the festival

Caelid is dangerous early. Prepare for Scarlet Rot, then challenge Starscourge Radahn at Redmane Castle when ready. His defeat opens the way to Nokron.

  • Continue Ranni's quest in Nokron to obtain the Fingerslayer Blade.
  • Use Flame, Cleanse Me or boluses for Rot-heavy areas.
  • Suggested range: level 60–80.
04 · ALTUS & GELMIR

Approach the capital

Reach Altus with both Dectus Medallion halves or through the Ruin-Strewn Precipice. Explore Mt. Gelmir and Volcano Manor before entering Leyndell.

  • Volcano Manor contracts are optional; Rykard can be defeated without completing all of them.
  • Collect Golden Seeds and Sacred Tears while crossing Altus.
  • Suggested range: level 70–100.
05 · LEYNDELL

Claim the capital

Enter Leyndell after claiming two Great Runes. Defeat Morgott to receive the Rold Medallion and gain access to the Mountaintops.

  • Explore the Royal Capital before the final boss of Crumbling Farum Azula changes it.
  • Finish capital pickups and NPC conversations you do not want to miss.
  • Suggested range: level 90–110.
06 · MOUNTAINTOPS

Decide what to finish

Cross the Forbidden Lands to the Mountaintops and defeat the Fire Giant. Before using the Forge, finish any remaining Leyndell exploration and side quests.

  • Haligtree is an optional, high-difficulty endgame area; find both Secret Medallion halves.
  • Using the Forge advances the story; Maliketh later transforms the capital.
  • Suggested range: level 100–130.
07 · FARUM AZULA

Face the final path

Complete Crumbling Farum Azula and defeat Maliketh. Return to the changed capital and make your way through the final encounters.

  • Finish remaining quests and collectables before defeating Maliketh.
  • After the final boss, choose an available ending interaction.
  • Suggested range: level 120–150 with a well-upgraded weapon.

Before moving on: Major story events can move NPCs and alter Leyndell. If a quest matters to you, exhaust dialogue, revisit the character's last location, and complete the quest before the Forge/Maliketh milestones.

Stories across the Lands Between

NPC quests

Characters move after key events. Talk until dialogue repeats, then check the next listed location. Spoiler details are in the expandable cards.

ENDING · RANNI Ranni the Witch Caria Manor · Nokron · Ainsel River
  1. Meet Ranni at Ranni's Rise after clearing Caria Manor; agree to serve her.
  2. Speak with Blaidd, Iji, and Seluvis at the tower, then meet Blaidd in Siofra River.
  3. Defeat Radahn; enter the crater in Limgrave and retrieve the Fingerslayer Blade from Nokron.
  4. Give the blade to Ranni. Continue through Renna's Rise, Ainsel River, Nokstella, and the Lake of Rot.
  5. Defeat Astel, use the Dark Moon Ring at the Cathedral of Manus Celes, and finish her quest.
  6. After the final boss, use Ranni's blue summon sign to choose her ending.
ENDING · FIA Fia, Deathbed Companion Roundtable Hold · Deeproot Depths
  1. Accept Fia's embrace at the Roundtable Hold and exhaust her dialogue.
  2. Give D the Weathered Dagger when Fia offers it; reload the Hold and find the aftermath.
  3. Progress through Ranni's quest until you can obtain the Cursemark of Death.
  4. Reach Deeproot Depths, speak to Fia, and defeat the champions.
  5. Give Fia the Cursemark of Death and enter her dream to defeat Lichdragon Fortissax.
  6. Collect the Mending Rune of the Death-Prince; use it after the final boss for the Duskborn ending.
ENDING · DUNG EATER Loathsome Dung Eater Roundtable Hold · Leyndell sewers · Outer moat
  1. After reaching Altus, find his apparition at the Roundtable Hold and show him a Seedbed Curse to receive the Sewer-Gaol Key.
  2. Use the key to reach his cell in the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds beneath Leyndell. Speak to him and choose to release him.
  3. Read his message at the Roundtable Hold, then meet and defeat his invasion at the outer moat in Leyndell.
  4. Return to his cell and give him five Seedbed Curses, found across areas including Leyndell, Volcano Manor, and Elphael.
  5. Collect the Mending Rune of the Fell Curse from him; use it after the final boss for the Blessing of Despair ending.
QUEST · MILLICENT Millicent Caelid · Altus · Mountaintops · Haligtree
  1. Speak to Gowry in his shack, retrieve the Unalloyed Gold Needle from Commander O'Neil, and have it repaired.
  2. Give the needle to Millicent at the Church of the Plague; reload and speak with her again.
  3. Meet her at Gowry's Shack, then at the Erdtree-Gazing Hill after she leaves the church.
  4. Give her the Valkyrie's Prosthesis in the Shaded Castle; speak to her at Windmill Village after defeating the Godskin Apostle.
  5. Talk to her at the Ancient Snow Valley Ruins and then at the Prayer Room in Elphael.
  6. After defeating the Ulcerated Tree Spirit in Elphael, choose the gold sign to assist her. Return to the pool afterward to recover the Unalloyed Gold Needle; use it to obtain Miquella's Needle for a late-game ending safeguard.
QUEST · ALEXANDER Iron Fist Alexander Limgrave · Gael Tunnel · Caelid · Farum Azula
  1. Free Alexander from the ground north of Agheel Lake; speak with him after helping.
  2. Meet him in Gael Tunnel and at the Radahn Festival. Speak with him after the battle.
  3. Find him in the lava near Fort Laiedd on Mt. Gelmir.
  4. Use an Oil Pot to free him in the Mountaintops, then speak with him.
  5. Meet him in Crumbling Farum Azula and accept his duel. His shard rewards skill-focused builds.
ENDING · VARRE White Mask Varre The First Step · Rose Church · Mohgwyn Palace
  1. Speak with Varre at The First Step, then again at Rose Church in Liurnia.
  2. Complete three invasions with his fingers; victories are not required. Offline alternative: defeat Magnus the Beast Claw at Writheblood Ruins using his red summon sign.
  3. Soak the Lord of Blood's Favor in a maiden's blood, then return to Varre.
  4. Accept the Pureblood Knight's Medal to reach Mohgwyn Palace early.
  5. Complete his invasion sign near the mausoleum and speak with him for his quest rewards.
ENDING · GOLDMASK Brother Corhyn & Goldmask Altus Plateau · Leyndell · Mountaintops
  1. After reaching Altus, find Corhyn there and meet the silent Goldmask on the Forest-Spanning Greatbridge.
  2. Speak to both, then meet them near the colosseum in Leyndell.
  3. Learn Law of Regression (requires 37 Intelligence) and cast it at the statue message in the capital; report the revelation to Goldmask.
  4. After the Forge, find them in the Mountaintops and later in the changed capital.
  5. Collect the Mending Rune of Perfect Order from Goldmask's body and use it after the final boss for that ending.

Choices leave their mark

Endings

There are six ending outcomes: the default choice, three Mending Rune variations, Ranni's path, and the Frenzied Flame. Complete the relevant quest before the final battle.

DEFAULT

Age of Fracture

Defeat the final boss and choose “Mend the Elden Ring.” No optional ending quest is required.

FIA

Age of the Duskborn

Complete Fia's quest and use the Mending Rune of the Death-Prince at the end.

GOLDMASK

Age of Order

Complete Corhyn and Goldmask's quest, including the Law of Regression clue, then use the Mending Rune of Perfect Order.

DUNG EATER

Blessing of Despair

Complete the Dung Eater's quest by giving him five Seedbed Curses; use the Mending Rune of the Fell Curse.

RANNI

Age of the Stars

Finish Ranni's quest. After the final boss, interact with her blue summon sign instead of the Elden Ring.

MISSABLE CHOICE

Lord of Frenzied Flame

Meet the Three Fingers beneath Leyndell. This locks other endings unless you complete Millicent's quest, obtain Miquella's Needle, and use it in Dragonlord Placidusax's arena.

Keep your options open: Do not accept the Three Fingers' embrace until you are sure. The other five endings can remain available together; after the final battle, choose only one for this playthrough.

Build with a purpose

Build basics

A reliable build starts with survivability, a weapon you enjoy, and enough attributes to use it. Tune the exact numbers in the planner.

Stay alive first

Vigor is valuable on every build. Many players aim for about 40 by midgame and 60 by endgame; treat these as useful targets, not rules.

Upgrade your weapon

Early weapon upgrades often add more damage than spreading levels across several offensive stats. Find smithing stones and improve one main weapon.

Choose one main damage stat

Meet weapon requirements first, then focus Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Faith, or Arcane according to its scaling and your playstyle.

Keep a medium load

Stay below 70% equip load for a medium roll. Endurance raises stamina and maximum equip load, so invest when armor or weapons make you heavy.
